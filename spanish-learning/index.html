Viaje Boricua

Un viaje por Puerto Rico para aprender español boricua.

An RPG that tricks you into learning Spanish. Start in Old San Juan, help the neighbors, then ride the guagua to new parts of the island, each with new words and Puerto Rican slang.

1Move with the Spanish buttons. The English hints disappear once you've used them enough.
2Tap any palabra to hear it and see what it means.
3English subtitles fade away after the first quests. Tap EN if you get stuck.
