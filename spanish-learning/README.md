# Viaje Boricua

A pixel-art RPG set in Old San Juan, Puerto Rico, that teaches Spanish while you play.
It's a single file: open `index.html` in any browser (it works on phones too).

- Move with buttons labeled in Spanish (`arriba`, `abajo`, `izquierda`, `derecha`). Each button's English hint disappears after you've pressed it 12 times.
- Tap any underlined word to hear it and see what it means. A word stops being underlined once you've seen it 6 times.
- English subtitles show for the first few quests, then go away. Tap **EN** in a dialog or the objective banner if you get stuck.
- The quest line: bring Abuela a mango, deliver a letter to the pink house, find Sofía's coquí under the blue bench, gather two coconuts for Don Luis, buy a lemon piragua, answer the guard's three questions, and climb El Morro to the flag.
- Progress is saved in the browser.
